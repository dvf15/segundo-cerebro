All events were classified as low risk. The agent applied specific operational rules throughout: no plaza left vacant for more than one consecutive week, montagem (setup) and desmontagem (teardown) windows built into scheduling, preference for events with sponsorship potential, and thematic alignment between retail calendar dates and event concepts (e.g., horror/Halloween, family/Children's Day, groove music/Father's Day).

In the follow-up exchange, the person requested a suggestion of operators (fornecedores/operadores) for the planned events. Claude responded with a transparency-first approach, explaining it could provide operator categories, selection criteria, and RFP guidance, but flagged a confidence limitation (6/10) around providing specific company names, noting that the SP events supplier market changes rapidly and that any named list risks being outdated. Claude asked two clarifying questions before proceeding: whether Mooca Plaza already had a homologated supplier registry through its administrator, and what the most critical selection criteria were (price, proven shopping-center experience, or sponsorship capture capability). The conversation ended at this clarifying stage, with no operator list yet delivered.
