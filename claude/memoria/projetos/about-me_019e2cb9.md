# Memória do projeto: About Me

**Purpose & context**

Daniel is a Brazilian real estate executive serving as Portfolio Director at Allos, one of Brazil's largest shopping mall companies, overseeing a portfolio of 10 shopping centers across four Brazilian states with 12 direct reports. His background spans economics (Mackenzie), an MBA (FGV), and specializations at Columbia, Stanford, Insper, and the Disney Institute, plus over two decades of leadership across AmBev, an American import/distribution company, Brasif Holding, and Allos. He has deep expertise in sector M&A, strategy, finance, commercial operations, and people management.

In parallel, Daniel is building a personal venture in agentic AI and has been an active angel investor since 2020, operating through a value/dividend-focused lens. His genuinely rare specific knowledge has been diagnosed as the intersection of shopping mall operator, sector M&A practitioner, active angel investor, and long-horizon capital allocator — not generic executive skills.

Personal interests include sailing, BJJ, fitness, travel, and investing.

**Current state**

Daniel is executing a career hedge strategy (v3) that treats his current employer as a leverage platform through internal IP development, rather than a solo exit consultancy. This approach was chosen specifically because a solo consultancy model raised conflict-of-interest concerns with his employer. A fallback plan (v2 — an ethically-gated solo model with event-based phase gates) is maintained as an executable alternative if the v3 internal pitch is rejected.

His leverage gaps are code and media, both intended to be addressed using his employer's existing labor and capital infrastructure under the v3 scenario. He has acknowledged having no technical building skills and has requested a concrete path to develop them via existing AI tools.

**On the horizon**

- 30-day operational tracking underway via Obsidian Tasks plugin
- Decision point: whether the v3 internal IP development pitch is accepted by his employer (determines whether v2 fallback activates)
- Ongoing development of his agentic AI personal venture

**Key learnings & principles**

- Adjusting timelines alone does not resolve a conflict of interest — this requires a fundamentally different strategic posture
- Internal IP development (v3) represents a categorically different strategy than a solo exit hedge (v1/v2), not a refinement of it
- Preserving historical reasoning across plan versions is valuable for decision accountability
- Daniel has accepted structural pushbacks from Claude when well-reasoned, consistently preferring honest assessments over optimistic framing

**Approach & patterns**

Daniel has established explicit operating rules for Claude:

- **Ask clarifying questions before acting** on ambiguous or complex requests; never assume missing context
- **Reflect understanding back** before delivering outputs on complex tasks
- **Challenge weak reasoning and fragile plans directly** — prioritize accuracy and honest pushback over agreeableness
- **Express uncertainty explicitly** rather than guessing; attach a confidence score (1–10) to responses, flagging anything below 7
- **Cite verifiable sources** for factual claims involving numbers, statistics, people, or quotes

Daniel works iteratively, accepts well-reasoned structural pushbacks, and prefers plans organized around event-based phase gates rather than calendar milestones.
