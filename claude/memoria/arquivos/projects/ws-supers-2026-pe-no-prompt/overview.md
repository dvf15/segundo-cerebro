## Purpose & context

- [stated] Daniel is building an internal workshop for shopping mall superintendents that introduces AI tools to commercial teams.
- [stated] In parallel he is developing a practical AI agent called "Zél" — a commercial prospecting agent designed to identify, qualify, and initiate contact with potential tenants within a defined radius of a mall.
- [stated] Zél is conceptually framed as a fusion of two real-world figures: Sam Zell (American investor, author of *Am I Being Too Subtle?*), who contributes mindset/mentalidade feeding the system prompt context; and Zé Roberto Levy (shopping mall commercial executive), who contributes execution/execução feeding the agent's operational skills.
- [stated] Levy's 12 mandamentos cover Brazilian shopping mall field prospecting practices: monitoring competitor malls, tracking expansion signals, leveraging multi-brand/multi-franchisee operators, maintaining a cadence of prospecting meetings, and similar.
- [stated] The workshop blends conceptual framing with hands-on group dynamics and live AI testing on Claude.ai, targeting a practical, informal tone with a caricature mascot (Zél) appearing throughout the materials.

## Current state

- [stated] Three workshop deliverables are complete.
- [stated] Deliverable 1 — PowerPoint deck of 14 slides in a corporate sober style (light background, charcoal plus amber/copper palette, Georgia/Calibri typography). It includes: a Zél DNA slide, Zell and Levy principle slides, a logic slide showing how each maps to agent design, a 60-minute group dynamic with three timed stages (context building → skill design → live Claude.ai testing), a blank worksheet template, a worked example using Levy mandamento #4, and a closing quote. The Zél JPEG mascot appears as a corner image across slides.
- [stated] Deliverable 2 — a 90-second video opening produced for NotebookLM, including a timed narration script, scene-by-scene storyboard, colour/typography specs matching the PPT, audio/SFX direction, and CapCut/Canva assembly instructions.
- [stated] Deliverable 3 — the Hunter Agent specification, covering mission, system prompt with Sam Zell principles, six operational skills, a scoring rubric, approach scripts, and ecosystem integration.

## Key learnings & principles

- [stated] The dual-parent framing (Zell = mindset → prompt; Levy = execution → skills) is a core pedagogical device that makes the agent's design logic legible to non-technical audiences.
- [stated] Workshop tone is deliberately informal to match the audience and encourage participation.
- [stated] Iterative PDF-to-image QA was essential for catching visual defects such as overlapping text, clipped elements, card overflow, and image proportion issues.
