## Purpose and method

- [stated] Daniel is building a retirement-oriented investment portfolio focused on Brazilian stocks in stable sectors (utilities, financials, energy transmission, sanitation, insurance)
- [stated] Core valuation framework is the Bazin method: fair price = 5-year average dividends per share divided by 0.06 (a 6% minimum dividend yield threshold)
- [stated] Sells put options as a "synthetic dividend" strategy to generate income alongside traditional dividends

## Stocks tracked

- [stated] Currently tracks KLBN4 (Klabin), ITSA4 (Itaúsa), BBDC4 (Bradesco), and AXIA3 (Axia Energia)
- [stated] AXIA3 is flagged as unsuitable for Bazin analysis — its 2022 privatization makes the 5-year dividend history misleading
- [stated] BBDC4's dividend average is lower-confidence: trailing 12-month figures were used instead of a true 5-year average, due to data limitations

## Known limitations and next steps (current state)

- [stated] The current four-stock portfolio carries concentration risk: two correlated financials, one cyclical commodity, and one with a limited private-sector track record
- [stated] Diversification across uncorrelated stable sectors — sanitation, energy transmission, insurance — is a known improvement area and the likely next move
- [stated] BBDC4 and AXIA3 Bazin inputs may be refined as better historical dividend data becomes available
- [stated] AXIA3's post-privatization history is a structural limitation for any yield-history-based valuation method, not just Bazin
