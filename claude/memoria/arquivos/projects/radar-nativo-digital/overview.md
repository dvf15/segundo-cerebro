- [stated] Daniel is conducting structured market research on Brazilian fashion and apparel brands across multiple niches, including men's fashion, fitness/bodybuilding, and combat sports fightwear
- [stated] The work is oriented toward building vetted brand pipelines, likely for commercial, investment, or partnership purposes
- [stated] Success looks like clean, sourced spreadsheets with high data integrity rather than inflated lists
- [stated] Domain areas involved: Brazilian DTC/apparel market, Instagram-based brand discovery, cluster/positioning analysis (AB medium-high), and regional retail context such as Iguatemi POA/Brasília

## Current state — completed research tracks

- [stated] Three research tracks have been completed, each resulting in a formatted Excel deliverable
- [stated] Men's fashion: 12 verified brands meeting relaxed criteria (post-2016, 15K+ followers, Regional premium cluster); top fits identified as Sardegna Sartoria, CARNAN, and P. Andrade
- [stated] Bodybuilding (musculação): ~19–20 brands across confidence tiers, with only one fully meeting all four original criteria; overall confidence rated 7/10
- [stated] Combat sports fightwear: 13 validated Brazilian-owned brands (down from a 20-brand target), ranked by followers from KVRA (235K) to Naja Extreme (17K); the gap was explained by foreign ownership of well-known brands
- [stated] In all three cases the original target of 20 brands could not be met without compromising data integrity, and Daniel consistently accepted honest shortfalls over inflated results

## On the horizon

- [stated] Potential expansion of the fightwear list by either lowering the follower threshold to 5K or broadening scope to athleisure brands with combat-sport aesthetics
- [stated] Potential relaxation of the founding-year cutoff for the bodybuilding segment (≥2015 recommended) to yield a more actionable pipeline
- [stated] Additional niche segments may be researched following the same methodology
